Scan for broken and redirected links

Fetches every published article from the AppsFlyer Help Center, extracts every hyperlink, then checks each URL — flagging broken links (404/5xx), redirects (3xx), network errors, and links that could not be verified due to browser security restrictions (CORS). Runs entirely in your browser.

Checks all unique URLs across all articles. Time depends on the number of unique links — typically 10–20 min for a full KB scan. Results are cached so you don't need to re-run every session.

Consistency — Missing References

Each section in the Help Center should have one overview or guide article that links to everything else in that section. This scan checks: does every published, public article in a section appear in the section's overview? If not, that article is a gap — it exists, but users who land on the overview will never know it's there.

Only scans published articles visible to all users. Skips WIP, Beta, and draft content. Mark gaps as Reviewed to hide them — they won't reappear on the next scan.
